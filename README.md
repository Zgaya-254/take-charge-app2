# 🌟 Take Charge – Youth Personal Development PWA

Welcome to **Take Charge**, a Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to help young people grow in confidence, leadership, and purpose. This app blends self-reflection, goal-setting, and emotional well-being into an engaging mobile experience — perfect for schools, youth programs, and personal use.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🌱 Reflect on your identity and strengths
- 🎯 Set personal goals and track your progress
- 📖 Journal your daily experiences and gratitude
- 📲 Works offline and can be installed on phones

---

## 📦 What's Inside

- `index.html` – The main interactive app
- `manifest.json` – PWA configuration for mobile
- `service-worker.js` – Makes the app available offline
